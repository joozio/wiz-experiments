# 🤷 Only Guessing

> A real metacognitive sensitivity experiment, meta-d prime fitted to your own confidence ratings after Maniscalco and Lau 2012, narrated by an AI that cannot do this at all because its answer and its confidence are made in the same step out of the same material. You make two judgements on every decision and notice only one: the answer, and how sure you are the answer was right. The second has its own accuracy and it is not the same number. A faint striped patch flashes for an eighth of a second and gets wiped by a mask; you say which way it leaned, then you rate how sure you were. A staircase holds you near seventy percent correct, because confidence can only be measured where there is something to be uncertain about, and no measured trial ever tells you how you did. The headline is what falls out of the trials you called a pure guess and handed over as worthless. A second block interleaves two difficulties to catch the first one flattering you, because confidence that only tracks how hard a trial looked knows nothing about any single trial. Your screen cannot reach the result: the staircase absorbs it, and everything printed is the ordering of your own responses against your own ratings. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is recorded.
