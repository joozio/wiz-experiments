# \u{1F521} The Company It Keeps

> A real Reicher and Wheeler experiment, run on your own eyes, narrated by an AI that has never seen a letter in its life. Four characters flash for a few hundredths of a second, a pattern mask lands on top of them, and two letters appear under one position. You pick the one that was there. People are better at this when the four characters spelled a word, and the usual explanation is that you guessed the letter from the rest. That explanation is the thing this page removes. Both offered letters always make a word, so the choice is between WORD and WORK, and knowing English perfectly is worth exactly nothing. The comparison is not against some other string either. It is against the same four letters with the probed one left exactly where it was and the other three shuffled into an order English does not allow. Same letters, same position on the screen, same two buttons, same exposure measured in whole frames, zero guessing value on both sides. The only thing that differs is the order of three letters you were never asked about. Then the page stops, because two explanations survive everything above and they were the real argument of the nineteen seventies. Either the advantage comes from the string being a word, or it comes from the string being spelled the way English spells things. They come apart on one thing: a pseudoword, which is not a word and never was, and is perfectly spelled. Your own numbers are used to compute what each explanation predicts, both go on the screen, and only then does the pseudoword block open. The verdict rests on how often a bootstrap resample put your pseudowords at the word end rather than the scramble end, and a result landing between the two rivals is called a draw rather than rounded toward the nearer one. The page also prints how often it is wrong. Simulated against three hundred visitors in each of five worlds with known truth, it calls a real fourteen point advantage forty seven percent of the time and invents one out of nothing one percent of the time, and both numbers are on the results page instead of hidden. A one button responder, a guesser, a machine speed responder, a hidden tab and a paused display are each refused with the cause named. Everything runs in your browser, nothing is recorded, nothing leaves the page.
